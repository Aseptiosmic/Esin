Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film interference shifts hue across grazing angles, turning dark edges violet and emerald as a shard tips toward the key light.

Index of refraction — The ratio that bends transmitted rays at the boundary; higher IOR deepens internal reflections and compresses the world behind the slab.

Dispersion — Wavelength-dependent refraction splits white highlights into spectral fringes along bevels and cut seams via KHR_materials_dispersion.

Transmission — Energy that passes through the volume after Fresnel reflection, attenuated over thickness so deep cuts read as smoked charcoal.

Roughness — Microfacet scatter on both the base surface and the clearcoat layer, balancing crisp rim speculars against soft studio gradients.
