# Archived design references

The previous Erathra library and its prompts are intentionally retained in this
directory. They are not imported by the Ela website and are not linked in its UI.

Sources researched while art-directing the Ela page:

- Awwwards, "OREO & BTS - Love Letter Site": personalized letters as an experience, not a collection of generic UI widgets. https://www.awwwards.com/sites/oreo-bts-love-letter-site
- Awwwards, "Love letters to cities": restrained museum-like typography and photography. https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/home-white-love-letters-to-cities-thyssen
- Awwwards, "Red Collar's Endless Letter": motion tied to the act of reading and opening a letter. https://www.awwwards.com/case-study-red-collars-endless-letter-website.html
- Digital Love Letter: an editorial full-bleed image and private, personal copy over decorative effects. https://digitalloveletter.com/

Existing archived site ideas adapted for Ela:

- `orvante/`: deliberate editorial scale and generous negative space.
- `vasari/`: the emotional effect of opening a physical object to reveal text.
- `wild/`: a single visual reveal with a restrained interaction, rather than many decorative effects.

Ela deliberately does not invent shared memories, collect responses, or attempt
to persuade through deceptive button behavior. The first section is a full-bleed
photographic setting; the letter is opened by the recipient; the last question
offers honest choices with equal respect.

Personal context supplied by the sender:
- Sender: Kadir. Recipient: Ela. Both are 14 and in class 9A.
- No shared photographs or specific memories were supplied, so none are fabricated.
- The invitation is age-appropriate: getting to know each other and talking more
  at school. There is no coffee/date framing.
- Ela already has Kadir's number. The site therefore offers an editable reply,
  copy-to-clipboard, and a generic WhatsApp share link (wa.me/?text=...). It never
  sends a message automatically and stores no answer.
- Visual direction for this revision: school notebook, yellow envelope, powder
  blue paper, daisy yellow, forest-green ink; Bricolage Grotesque + DM Sans +
  Caveat. The earlier serif/cream direction was replaced.
